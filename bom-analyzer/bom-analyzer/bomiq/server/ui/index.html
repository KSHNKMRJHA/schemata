Drop a BOM here

BOM-IQ reads the file, works out which column is which, cleans and validates the data, then looks every part up across your distributor APIs to price it, score its supply risk, check compliance and find alternates. Everything runs on this machine.

Any template

Altium, OrCAD, KiCad, Mentor, SAP, Arena, a hand-made spreadsheet — columns are matched by name, by pattern and by what the values actually look like. Confirm once and the layout is remembered.

Seven data sources

DigiKey, Mouser, Octopart/Nexar, Arrow, Farnell/element14, LCSC and TrustedParts, queried in parallel and merged. No keys yet? The built-in offline catalogue drives the whole pipeline.

Decisions, not just data

Lifecycle and EOL risk, stock against your build, MOQ-aware costing, cheapest sourcing basket, RoHS/REACH/export flags and scored alternates — with the reasoning shown for every number.
